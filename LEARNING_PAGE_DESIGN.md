## 🎯 Modern Design Features

1. **Glassmorphism** - Backdrop blur on cards and inputs
2. **Gradient Accents** - Blue-to-indigo gradients throughout
3. **Shadow Depth** - Layered shadows for visual hierarchy
4. **Smooth Transitions** - 300ms transitions on interactions
5. **Color Coding** - Intuitive colors for different states
6. **Responsive Design** - Works seamlessly on mobile to desktop
7. **Icon Integration** - Meaningful icons throughout
8. **Typography** - Clear hierarchy with bold titles
9. **Spacing** - Consistent spacing with Tailwind
10. **Focus States** - Visible focus indicators for accessibility
